FitBench Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What FitBench collects

Nothing. FitBench does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your data files

Data you open, paste or type into FitBench is processed entirely on your own device. It is never uploaded to a server. Files you export are saved only where you choose to save them.

Network access

FitBench connects to the internet only to download the app itself, its updates, and its display fonts (from Google Fonts). After the first launch it works offline.

Children

FitBench collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact details on the FitBench page in the Microsoft Store.
